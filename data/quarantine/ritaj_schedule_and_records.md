## Viewing your class schedule (SAMPLE — verify in Ritaj)

Your weekly class schedule for the current term is shown on the Ritaj portal once
registration is complete:

1. Log in to **ritaj.birzeit.edu** with your university account.
2. Open the **Schedule** (timetable) view for the current term.
3. Each entry shows the course code, section, room, day and time.

The schedule updates automatically whenever you add or drop a course during the
add/drop period.

## Viewing your exam schedule (SAMPLE — verify in Ritaj)

The midterm and final exam timetable is published on Ritaj for the registered
term. Check the exam date, time and room for each course there, and review the
hall instructions before the exam day.

## Viewing grades and your academic record (SAMPLE — verify in Ritaj)

1. Log in to **ritaj.birzeit.edu**.
2. Open the **Academic Records** module.
3. Select **Grades** and choose the term to see your marks, or open your full
   academic record to see completed courses, credit hours and your GPA.

Grades are released by each instructor after the term; your cumulative GPA is
computed on the 4-point scale (see the grading document).

## Requesting an official transcript (REAL)

Unofficial grades are visible on Ritaj, but an **official transcript** is issued
by the Registration & Admission Office. Request official documents through Ritaj
or by visiting the office; you pay for requested papers at the Finance
Department. See https://www.birzeit.edu/en/admissions/certificates-and-documents.
