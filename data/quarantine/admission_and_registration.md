## Admission process for new bachelor's students

After being accepted, complete these steps:

1. Receive your **acceptance letter**.
2. Pay the **tuition / seat-reservation fee** (the seat-reservation fee is later
   deducted from your total tuition).
3. Collect your **university ID**.
4. **Register for the English level (placement) test.**
5. Submit your required documents to the Registration & Admission Office.

## Required documents

- A copy of your **personal ID**.
- A copy of your **birth certificate**.
- A **certified copy of the General Secondary Certificate** (Tawjihi / high-school
  exam result).
