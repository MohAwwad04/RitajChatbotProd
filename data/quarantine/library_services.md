## The Main Library (REAL)

The **Yusuf Ahmed Alghanim Library** is Birzeit University's main library. It
serves students, staff and researchers with print collections, study space, and
access to academic databases of e-books and full-text journals. General library
information is at https://www.birzeit.edu/en/content/main-library and
https://www.birzeit.edu/en/study/bzu-library.

## Finding books and resources (REAL)

- Search the **online catalog** to locate books and check availability.
- The library provides access to **electronic databases**, e-books and full-text
  journals for coursework and research.
- It is a depository for UNESCO publications and a regional center for World Bank
  publications.

## Using the library (SAMPLE — verify with the library)

- Borrow and return physical items with your **university ID**.
- Use your university credentials for off-campus access to licensed databases.
- Ask library staff or the reference desk for help locating materials or using
  the databases.

## Who to contact

- **Main Library (BZU Library):** borrowing, catalog, databases, and research
  help. See https://www.birzeit.edu/en/study/bzu-library.
