# Birzeit University — Tuition and Fees

> SOURCE: REAL — public information from the Birzeit University Tuition Fees page
> (https://www.birzeit.edu/en/admissions/tuition-fees-0), collected June 2026.
> Fees can change annually by official university decision; confirm current rates
> before paying.

## Cost per credit hour

- **Most programs:** JD 125 per credit hour.
- **MBA program:** JD 160 per credit hour.

Tuition is **calculated after registration is complete**, based on the number of
credit hours you register for that term. Currency is the Jordanian Dinar (JD).

## Seat reservation (new students)

New students pay a **seat-reservation fee** to hold their place. This fee is
**deducted from the total tuition** and **includes the Arabic and English level
(placement) tests**.

## Notes

- Tuition rates can change annually based on an official decision by the
  university administration.
- For an itemized cost estimate, contact the Registration and Admission
  department (phone +970-2-2982057 / +970-2-2982056 / +970-2-2982054).
