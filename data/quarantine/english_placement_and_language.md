# Birzeit University — English Placement & Language Program

> SOURCE: REAL — public information from Birzeit University admissions and English
> program pages (https://www.birzeit.edu/en/admissions/new-students-admission/admission-process
> and /students/english-language-instruction-program-unlock), collected June 2026.

## English level (placement) test

All new students must **register for and sit the English level (placement) test**
as part of admission, after receiving the acceptance letter and visiting the
Registration & Admission Office. The fee for the Arabic and English level tests
is included in the seat-reservation fee.

## Levels and the CEFR

Birzeit's English language instruction is aligned with the **Common European
Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR)**, which defines six levels from
**A1** (basic user) through **C2** (proficient user).

## Placement outcome (example)

- Students who score **38–55** on the university placement test are placed into
  **ENGC 102**.

(Other score bands map to other ENGC courses; confirm your exact placement with
the English Language Center / your placement result.)
