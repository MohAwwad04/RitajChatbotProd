## Academic advising (SAMPLE — verify with your department)

Each student has an **academic advisor** in their department who helps plan the
study program, choose courses each term, and stay on track with prerequisites
and degree requirements. Meet your advisor before registration to confirm which
courses to take next.

## Tracking progress to graduation (SAMPLE — verify in Ritaj)

Your **academic record** on Ritaj shows completed courses and credit hours
against your program's requirements. Use it, with your advisor, to see what
remains: major requirements, electives, and any project or capstone.

## Applying to graduate (SAMPLE — verify with Registration)

When you are close to finishing:

1. Confirm with your **advisor** that all degree requirements are met.
2. Submit a **graduation request** to the **Registration & Admission Office** for
   the term you expect to finish.
3. Clear any financial or library holds so your record can be finalized.
4. Registration audits your record and confirms graduation; official certificates
   and transcripts are then requested through Registration.
