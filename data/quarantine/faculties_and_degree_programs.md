## Faculties (English)

Birzeit University is organized into nine faculties (كليات):

1. **Faculty of Arts** — established 1972, one of the university's first two
   faculties (humanities, languages, social sciences).
2. **Faculty of Science** — established 1972 (mathematics, physics, chemistry,
   biology and related sciences).
3. **Faculty of Business and Economics** — business administration, accounting,
   economics, finance and marketing programs.
4. **Faculty of Engineering and Technology** — formed in 2014 by merging the
   Faculty of Engineering with the Faculty of Information Technology; includes
   engineering disciplines plus computer science and computer engineering.
5. **Faculty of Law and Public Administration** — law and public
   administration programs.
6. **Faculty of Education** — became a faculty at the start of the 2010/2011
   academic year, growing out of the Department of Education and Psychology.
7. **Faculty of Pharmacy, Nursing and Health Professions** — pharmacy, nursing
   and allied health programs.
8. **Faculty of Art, Music and Design** — the newest faculty, established at
   the beginning of the 2017–2018 academic year (design, visual art, music and
   related creative fields).
9. **Faculty of Graduate Studies (and Research)** — established 1996; runs the
   university's master's and doctoral programs.

Across these faculties the university offers roughly 76 bachelor's programs,
around 39 master's programs, and a small number of PhD programs (including
Social Sciences and Computer Science/Mathematics). The full, current catalog of
programs and their admission averages is on the university site under
Study → Programs, and course offerings for each semester appear in Ritaj.

## الكليات (بالعربية)

تضم جامعة بيرزيت تسع كليات: الآداب، والعلوم، والأعمال والاقتصاد، والهندسة
والتكنولوجيا، والحقوق والإدارة العامة، والتربية، والصيدلة والتمريض والمهن
الصحية، والفنون والموسيقى والتصميم، وكلية الدراسات العليا. وتطرح الجامعة
نحو ٧٦ برنامج بكالوريوس وحوالي ٣٩ برنامج ماجستير وعدداً من برامج الدكتوراه.
القائمة الكاملة والمحدّثة للبرامج وشروط القبول موجودة على موقع الجامعة، أما
المساقات المطروحة كل فصل فتظهر في بوابة ريتاج.
