## Your Ritaj account (REAL)

Your **university username and password** are issued by the Computer Center when
your account is created. The same credentials sign you in to Ritaj and to your
university email. **Never share your password** with anyone.

## Forgot password or locked account (SAMPLE — verify with the Computer Center)

1. Go to **ritaj.birzeit.edu**.
2. If you cannot sign in, contact the **IT helpdesk / Computer Center** and verify
   your identity with your **student ID**.
3. The Computer Center resets your password or unlocks the account.

After too many failed attempts an account may be temporarily locked; the
Computer Center clears the lock once your identity is confirmed.

## Keeping your account secure

- Choose a password you do not reuse elsewhere and do not share it.
- The university will not ask for your password by email; treat such messages as
  phishing and report them to IT Services.

## Who to contact

- **Computer Center / IT helpdesk:** password reset, locked accounts, and all
  Ritaj/email access problems. See https://www.birzeit.edu/en/it-services.
