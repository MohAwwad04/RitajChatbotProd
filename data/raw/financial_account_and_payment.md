## Your financial account on Ritaj (SAMPLE — verify in Ritaj)

Each student has a financial account on the Ritaj portal showing tuition charged
for the term, payments made, and any outstanding balance:

1. Log in to **ritaj.birzeit.edu**.
2. Open the **Financial Account** (finance) view.
3. Review charges, payments and the current balance owed.

## How tuition is charged (REAL)

Tuition is **calculated after registration is complete**, based on the number of
credit hours you register that term, at the per-credit-hour rate for your
program (Jordanian Dinar). New students first pay a seat-reservation fee that is
later deducted from the total tuition. See the tuition document for current
rates.

## Paying and clearing a balance (SAMPLE — verify with Finance)

- An **unpaid balance can place a hold** that blocks registration or document
  requests until it is settled.
- Pay through the channels approved by the **Finance Department**; the department
  posts the payment to your account.
- Payments for **requested official documents** are made at the Finance
  Department before the documents are issued.

## Who to contact

- **Finance Department:** balances, payments, and document-fee payment.
- **Registration & Admission:** tuition estimates and registration holds
