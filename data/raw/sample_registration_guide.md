## How to add or drop a course

1. Log in to Ritaj with your university account.
2. Open the **Registration** module from the main menu.
3. Choose **Add/Drop Courses** for the current term.
4. To add a course, search by course code (for example COMP2310) and select an
   open section, then confirm.
5. To drop a course, find it in your registered list and choose **Drop**.
6. Review your updated schedule and confirm the changes.

The add/drop period for the Fall term runs during the first two weeks of
classes. After this period closes, dropping a course is recorded as a
withdrawal and may appear on your transcript.

## How to view your grades

1. Log in to Ritaj.
2. Open the **Academic Records** module.
3. Select **Grades** and choose the term you want to view.

## How to request an official transcript

Submit a transcript request through the Registration office. Official
transcripts are issued by the Registration office and may take a few business
days to process.

## Who to contact

- Registration office: for add/drop, transcripts, and registration holds.
- IT helpdesk / Computer Center: for Ritaj login and account problems.
