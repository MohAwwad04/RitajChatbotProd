## Your university email (REAL)

Every student gets a **university email account**, provisioned together with your
Ritaj account by the Computer Center. Official university messages and many
notifications are sent to this address, so check it regularly.

## Accessing webmail (SAMPLE — verify with the Computer Center)

1. Log in to **ritaj.birzeit.edu** with your university account.
2. Open the **Email / Webmail** link on the portal to read and send mail.
3. Use the same university username and password that you use for Ritaj.

## Common email issues (SAMPLE — verify with the Computer Center)

- **Can't sign in to email:** the email account shares your Ritaj credentials, so
  fixing your Ritaj login usually fixes email; contact the Computer Center if it
  does not.
- **Mailbox or delivery problems:** the Computer Center (IT Services) handles
  university email. See https://www.birzeit.edu/en/it-services.

## Who to contact

- **Computer Center / IT helpdesk:** university email, webmail access, and
  account problems.
