## Financial aid at Birzeit (English)

Birzeit University runs a substantial aid program: roughly **30% of students
benefit** from some form of university aid. The **Financial Aid Unit**
(established 2017) advises students on the available programs.

Main forms of support:

- **Need-based scholarships and grants** — most scholarships are awarded on
  financial need. Selection starts with the student filling out a **Financial
  Aid Application on the Ritaj portal**, describing their economic situation
  and family income sources.
- **Merit-based scholarships** — for academic excellence, funded from the
  university's annual budget.
- **Faculty and program scholarships**, scholarships for **dependents of
  faculty and staff**, and the **Ramallah and Al-Bireh Governorate
  scholarships**, among others.
- **External and endowment scholarships** — e.g. the Sukhtian Foundation offers
  annual bachelor's-degree scholarships through the Birzeit University
  Endowment; other donor-funded scholarships are announced during the year.
- **Student loans** — the Loan Fund for Undergraduates in Palestine (**Iqrad**)
  grants loans that graduates repay later under flexible conditions.

The total yearly cost of study (tuition plus related expenses) has been
estimated at around $4,000 for a typical student, varying by program — see the
tuition and fees information for per-credit-hour prices.

**How to apply:** sign in to Ritaj, open the Financial Aid Application when the
application window is announced, fill in your household information, and follow
up with the Financial Aid Unit / Deanship of Student Affairs.

## المنح والمساعدات المالية (بالعربية)

توفر جامعة بيرزيت برامج مساعدات واسعة، إذ يستفيد نحو ٣٠٪ من الطلبة من شكل من
أشكال الدعم. تشمل المساعدات: منحاً على أساس الحاجة المالية (التقديم يبدأ بتعبئة
**طلب المساعدة المالية عبر بوابة ريتاج**)، ومنح التفوق الأكاديمي، ومنح الكليات
والبرامج، ومنح أبناء العاملين، ومنح محافظة رام الله والبيرة، ومنحاً خارجية مثل
منح مؤسسة سختيان، إضافة إلى قروض صندوق **إقراض** لطلبة البكالوريوس التي تُسدَّد
بعد التخرج بشروط ميسّرة. للاستفسار راجع وحدة المساعدات المالية أو عمادة شؤون
الطلبة.
