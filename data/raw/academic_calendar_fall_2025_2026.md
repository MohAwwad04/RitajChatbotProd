# Birzeit University — Academic Calendar (Fall 2025/2026)

> SOURCE: REAL — public information from the Birzeit University Academic Calendar
> page (https://www.birzeit.edu/en/study/academic-calendar), collected June 2026.
> Dates are for the Fall 2025/2026 semester. Always confirm current-term dates on
> the official calendar before acting; the university may revise them.

## Key dates — Fall semester 2025/2026

- **Mon, 22 September 2025** — Academic staff start of the Fall semester.
- **Mon, 29 September 2025** — Teaching (classes) begins.
- **Thu, 2 October 2025** — Last day of the add/drop and late-registration period.
- **Sat, 4 October 2025** — Withdrawal period begins; a course dropped from this
  date onward is recorded with a **"W"** (withdrawal) grade on the transcript.

## What the add/drop period means

During the add/drop and late-registration window (the first days of the
semester, ending 2 October 2025 for Fall 2025/2026) you can freely add courses,
drop courses, and change sections through the Ritaj portal with no transcript
record. After this window closes, leaving a course is a **withdrawal** and is
marked **"W"** on your record rather than simply removed.
