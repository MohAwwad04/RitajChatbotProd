# Birzeit University — Course Catalog, Programs & Departments

> SOURCE: REAL — public information from the Birzeit University Academic Programs
> and Academic Departments pages (https://www.birzeit.edu/en/study/programs and
> https://www.birzeit.edu/en/study/academic-departments), collected June 2026.
> The live course offering for a term (sections, seats, times) is shown inside the
> Ritaj portal after login.

## Academic programs (REAL)

Birzeit University offers a broad catalog of programs across the sciences,
engineering, business, arts, health and humanities, at the **Bachelor, Minor,
Master, Diploma and Ph.D.** levels. The full, browsable list of programs and the
degree type for each is published at
https://www.birzeit.edu/en/study/programs.

## Academic departments (REAL)

Programs are run by academic departments organized under the university's
faculties. The A–Z directory of departments is at
https://www.birzeit.edu/en/study/academic-departments — use it to find the
department that owns a course or program and its contact details.

## Browsing courses for a term (SAMPLE — verify in Ritaj)

The catalog of programs is public, but the **course offering for a specific term**
— which sections are open, their seats, times and instructors — is shown in the
Ritaj portal:

1. Log in to **ritaj.birzeit.edu**.
2. Open the **Registration** / course-browser view for the current term.
3. Search by course code (for example COMP2310) to see open sections before you
   register.
