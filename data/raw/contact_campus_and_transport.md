- For admission questions contact the **Registration & Admission Office**; for
  Ritaj login and account problems contact the **IT helpdesk / Computer
  Center**; for fees and payments contact the **Finance office**. Department
  extensions are reached through the main switchboard.

## Campus and getting to the university

The campus sits on the outskirts of Birzeit town, near Ramallah, about 20 km
northwest of Jerusalem. The main campus covers roughly 200 acres.

- **From Ramallah:** the Ramallah–Birzeit shared-taxi (service) line runs from
  Irsal Street in downtown Ramallah, a couple of minutes' walk from Al-Manara
  square, and stops at the university.
- Many students who live farther away rent apartments in Birzeit town or
  Ramallah; apartment buildings around campus largely serve students. The
  university built a women's dormitory (opened 2004); on-campus housing is
  limited, so most students arrange private housing nearby.
