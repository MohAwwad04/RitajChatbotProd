## What Ritaj is (REAL)

**Ritaj** is Birzeit University's electronic **academic and administrative
portal** for students, staff, and administration. It is reached at
**ritaj.birzeit.edu** and is where students register for courses, do add/drop,
view grades and schedules, and read official university messages/boards.

## Logging in to Ritaj (SAMPLE — verify with the Computer Center)

1. Go to **ritaj.birzeit.edu**.
2. Enter your **university username** and **password** (issued by the Computer
   Center when your account is created).
3. If you cannot log in or have forgotten your password, contact the IT helpdesk
   / Computer Center to reset it — do not share your password with anyone.

## Common IT issues (SAMPLE — verify with the Computer Center)

- **Forgot password / locked account:** contact the Computer Center helpdesk with
  your student ID to verify your identity and reset.
- **Student email:** your university email account is provisioned with your Ritaj
  account; access it through the link on the Ritaj portal.
- **Login or portal errors:** the Computer Center (IT Services) handles all Ritaj
  access and account problems. See https://www.birzeit.edu/en/it-services.

## Who to contact
