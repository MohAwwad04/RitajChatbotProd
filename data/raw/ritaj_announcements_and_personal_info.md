## Announcements and message boards (REAL)

Ritaj carries **official university messages and boards**. Course, department and
administrative announcements — deadlines, notices, and instructions — are posted
there, so check Ritaj regularly during the term. Important notices are also sent
to your university email.

## Reading your messages (SAMPLE — verify in Ritaj)

1. Log in to **ritaj.birzeit.edu**.
2. Open the **Messages / Boards** area to read announcements addressed to you and
   to your courses.

## Personal information (SAMPLE — verify in Ritaj)

Your Ritaj profile holds your personal and contact details on record with the
university:

1. Log in to **ritaj.birzeit.edu**.
2. Open the **Personal Info** view to review your details.
3. Some fields are editable in the portal; others (like legal name) are changed
   only through the Registration & Admission Office with documentation.

Keep your contact details current so you receive university notices.
