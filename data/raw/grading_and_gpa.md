## GPA scale (REAL)

- The grade-point average is computed on a **4-point scale** (out of 4).
- The average is **rounded to two decimal places**.

## Transcript / status codes (REAL)

- **W — Withdrawal:** recorded when you drop a course after the add/drop period
  closes.
- **WF — Withdrawal with Failure:** recorded when a student had a failing grade
  at the time their registration was suspended.

## Academic standing (SAMPLE — verify against official regulations)

- A cumulative GPA below the published minimum places a student on **academic
  probation**; sustained low performance can lead to dismissal. The exact
  thresholds are set by the official undergraduate regulations — confirm with the
  Registration office.
